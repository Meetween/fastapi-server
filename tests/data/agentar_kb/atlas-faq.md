# Atlas pilot, frequently asked questions

- The steering call meets every second Tuesday at 10:00 Central European Time.
- The support desk answers at atlas-support@example.org on working days from 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time.
- The pilot runs for six months after go live.
- The pilot counts as a success when 80 percent of the invited users are active every week.
- A training session lasts 90 minutes and takes at most 12 people.
- Usage logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted.
- The pilot release is version 0.9 and is labelled beta.
- All Atlas data is stored in the European Union only.
- Users get a one question survey every Friday.
- The pilot sponsor is Hanna Weber, Director of Operations.
